# 📊 ElectroHub Store — Sales Analysis 2025

## 📌 Project Overview

The **ElectroHub Store Sales Analysis** project is an Excel-based data analysis and business intelligence project created to analyze the sales performance of ElectroHub Store during 2025.

The project provides an overview of sales, customer behavior, delivery performance, product performance, sales channels, and profitability.

The analysis is presented using Excel data analysis, pivot tables, an interactive dashboard, and a business report.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Analyze sales across different platforms.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Analyze sales performance by city.
- Understand monthly sales trends.
- Analyze customer ratings.
- Evaluate delivery performance.
- Analyze profit margins.
- Provide useful business insights for decision-making.

The Excel workbook contains the following sheets:

### 4. DASHBOARD

Provides a visual overview of ElectroHub Store's 2025 sales performance.

- Categories
- Sales trends

- Delivery performance
- Profitability

### 5. REPORT

Contains the major findings and business conclusions from the analysis.

---

## 📊 Key Business Insights

According to the analysis:

- Total sales were approximately **₹43.60 Crore** from **1,560 orders**.
- **Amazon** generated the highest sales among the analyzed sales channels.
- **Laptops** were the best-selling product category.
- Sales peaked during **April, July, October and December**.
- The customer rating was **4.6**.
- The delivery success rate was **90%**.

## 📈 Business Analysis

### Sales Performance

The project analyzes overall sales and order performance to understand the company's business performance during 2025.

### Sales Channel Analysis

Sales are compared across different platforms such as:

- Amazon
- Croma
- Flipkart
- Snapdeal

This helps identify the sales channels contributing most to revenue.

### Product & Category Analysis

Products and categories are analyzed to identify high-performing products and categories.

The analysis identifies **Laptops** as the best-selling category.

### Time-Based Analysis

Monthly sales trends are analyzed to identify periods of higher sales.

The analysis shows sales peaks during:

- April
- July
- October
- December

### Customer Analysis

Customer age groups, ratings and purchasing behavior are analyzed to understand customer patterns.

### Delivery Analysis

Order status and delivery performance are analyzed to understand successful deliveries and identify opportunities for improvement.

### Profitability Analysis

Profit margin information is included in the dataset to help analyze the profitability of sales.
